01 / ABOUT ME

Design is more than how it looks. It is how it communicates.

I am John Baptist Byaruhanga, a creative professional from Kampala, Uganda, with a passion for visual communication, design and storytelling.

My experience includes graphic design, branding, printing, social media content, photography and video production.

I run Think Creative Solutions, where I help bring ideas to life through creative and practical visual solutions.

I am also pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Visual Effects and Animation at Victoria University, continuing to develop my skills in digital creativity and visual storytelling.
